What Specialties We Have

Frequently asked questions about our treatments and procedures.

Q. How can I prepare for an eye or cataract surgery?

Before surgery, avoid eating or drinking 6–8 hours prior. Arrange transport for the day of surgery. Bring any medications you currently take. Our team will provide detailed pre-operative instructions tailored to your specific procedure.

Q. How should I prepare for the root canal surgery?

Take any prescribed antibiotics or pain medications in advance. Eat a good meal before the appointment since your mouth will be numb afterward. Avoid alcohol 24 hours before treatment. Wear comfortable clothing and plan for some rest after the procedure.

Q. Questions about LASIK? What is the process?

LASIK is a quick 15-minute procedure. First, a detailed eye examination confirms candidacy. The laser reshapes the cornea under a sterile environment. You'll experience minimal discomfort and most patients see clearly within 24 hours with full recovery in 1–2 weeks.

Q. How long does a dental implant procedure take?

The implant placement itself takes 1–2 hours per tooth under local anesthesia. The complete process including healing and placing the crown typically takes 3–6 months. Our team ensures you have temporary teeth during this period for aesthetic and functional comfort.
